Song-Lyrics-Looper

A Python 3 script, standard library only, reads no input, asks nothing, and prints one fixed counting song about the three times table, then exits. The boards below are read once, in passing, which is all the attention this script asks.

Open the repo The proof board

How to run it

python3 songlyricslooper.py

No arguments prints the song and exits 0. The flags --help and -h print a one line usage note and exit 0. Any other argument prints the usage note to standard error and exits 2, with none of the song printed.

What comes out

A tally the visitor can follow line by line:

Chorus3 6 9 12 15 18 21 24 27 30, printed twice
Versescounting down from 3 times 10 is 30 through 3 times 2 is 6
Closing line3 times 1 is 3 of course
Chorus againtwice more

Forty one lines every run, four chorus lines in total, identical byte for byte between runs.

Proof

python3 -m unittest

Ten tests passing. They pin the full song text, the line count, the chorus count, the verse products in order, the closing line, the argument behaviour, and the fact that importing the module prints nothing.

The files

songlyricslooper.py, the script. test_songlyricslooper.py, the unittest suite. The GPL-3.0 licence. Logo and banner images. The song is also written out in the repo README, board by board.

The boards stay up for the next passer by. The count reaches thirty, comes back down to one, and the shallows are smooth again.
