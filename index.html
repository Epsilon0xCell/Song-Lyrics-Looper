Song-Lyrics-Looper

A Python 3 script, standard library only, reads no input, asks nothing, and prints one fixed counting song about the three times table, then exits. Like the pinned cylinder in a music box, it plays the same tune in the same order on every turn.

Open the repo The proof

How to run it

python3 songlyricslooper.py

No arguments prints the song and exits 0. The flags --help and -h print a one line usage note and exit 0. Any other argument prints the usage note to standard error and exits 2, with none of the song printed.

What comes out

A tally the visitor can follow line by line:

Chorus3 6 9 12 15 18 21 24 27 30, printed twice
Versescounting down from 3 times 10 is 30 through 3 times 2 is 6
Closing line3 times 1 is 3 of course
Chorus againtwice more

Forty one lines every run, four chorus lines in total, identical byte for byte between runs.

Proof

python3 -m unittest

Ten tests, all passing. They pin the full song text byte for byte, the 41 line count, the four chorus lines, the verse products in order, the closing line, the argument behaviour, and the fact that importing the module prints nothing.

The files

songlyricslooper.py, the script. test_songlyricslooper.py, the unittest suite. LICENSE, the GPL-3.0 licence. Logo and banner images. The song is also written out in the repo README, verse by verse.

Turn the crank and the count reaches thirty, comes back down to one, and starts again at exactly the same pin.
